# Design Notes

## Brand Colors
- Primary: Warm amber/gold (#f59e0b)
- Secondary: Deep stone (#1c1917)
- Accent: Soft cream (#fafaf9)

## Typography
- Headlines: Bold, modern sans-serif
- Body: Clean, readable

## Imagery Style
- Warm, inclusive photography
- Diverse representation
- Light, hopeful aesthetic
- Avoid overly religious imagery (crosses, etc.) - keep universal

## Slide Design Principles
1. One idea per slide
2. Minimal text, maximum impact
3. Data visualizations over bullet points
4. Consistent branding throughout
5. Leave white space

---

# Presentation Tips

1. **Open with story** - Personal anecdote about prayer/community
2. **Demo is key** - Show the app, don't just describe it
3. **Know your numbers** - Be ready for deep-dive questions
4. **Address the "religious" concern** - Emphasize universal appeal
5. **End with vision** - Paint the big picture
6. **Practice timing** - 10-12 minutes for pitch, rest for Q&A

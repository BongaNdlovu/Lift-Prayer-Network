Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 5, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Lift ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using Lift, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us:

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, and profile photo (optional)
  • Prayer Content: Prayer requests, testimonies, and comments you choose to share
  • Group Information: Groups you create or join, and group membership data
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and push notification tokens
  • Usage Data: App interactions, prayer counts, and engagement metrics

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Send push notifications for prayers, testimonies, and community updates
  • Enable community features like prayer groups and following
  • Moderate content to maintain a safe and respectful community
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and support requests

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Public Content: Prayer requests and testimonies you mark as "public" are visible to other users
  • Group Content: Content shared in groups is visible to group members
  • Service Providers: We use Firebase (Google) for authentication, database, and cloud services
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Secure authentication through Firebase Authentication
  • Encrypted data transmission using HTTPS/TLS
  • Firestore security rules to control data access
  • Regular security audits and updates

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: View your personal data through your profile settings
  • Update: Edit your profile information at any time
  • Delete: Delete your account and associated data from Settings
  • Privacy Controls: Set prayer requests as private or group-only
  • Notifications: Manage push notification preferences in Settings
  • Blocking: Block users whose content you don't want to see

Children's Privacy

Lift is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete such information.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. You can delete your account at any time, which will remove your personal data from our systems. Some information may be retained in backups for a limited period.

Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, cloud functions, and analytics
  • Expo: Push notification delivery
  • Sentry: Error tracking and crash reporting

These services have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

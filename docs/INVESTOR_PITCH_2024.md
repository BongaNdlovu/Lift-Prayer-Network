# Lift - Investment Pitch

## 🎯 The Problem

**Loneliness and spiritual disconnection are at epidemic levels:**
- 61% of adults report feeling lonely (Cigna, 2023)
- Mental health apps focus on self-help, not community
- Traditional religious institutions are losing younger generations
- Existing prayer apps are passive, lacking real engagement

---

## 💡 The Solution: Lift

**A social prayer network that transforms spiritual support from passive to active.**

Lift connects people through the universal act of prayer, creating meaningful micro-interactions that build community and provide measurable emotional support.

---

## 🚀 Key Value Propositions

### 1. **Network Effects in Faith**
Unlike meditation apps (solo experience), Lift is inherently social:
- Every prayer request creates engagement opportunities
- Following users builds sticky, personalized feeds
- Testimonies (answered prayers) create viral celebration moments
- Groups enable church/community-specific networks

### 2. **Engagement Metrics That Matter**
| Metric | Lift Advantage |
|--------|----------------|
| **DAU/MAU** | Prayer streaks drive daily returns |
| **Session depth** | Feed + notifications + groups = multiple touchpoints |
| **Retention** | Emotional investment in others' outcomes |
| **Virality** | "Pray for me" shares, testimony celebrations |

### 3. **Underserved $84B Market**
- Religious/spiritual apps market: $84B by 2028
- 2.4B Christians globally, 84% own smartphones
- Competitors (YouVersion, Hallow) focus on content, not community
- First-mover advantage in **social prayer**

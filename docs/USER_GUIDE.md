## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. **Open the app** and complete the onboarding tutorial
2. **Sign up** using:
   - Email and password
   - Google account
   - Continue as guest (limited features)
3. **Complete your profile** by adding your name and optional details

### Onboarding Questions

During setup, you'll answer a few questions to personalize your experience:
- Your faith journey stage
- How you prefer to pray
- What matters most to you (health, family, work, etc.)

## Home Feed

The home feed is your central hub for connecting with the prayer community.

### Two Tabs

| Tab | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **Prayer Requests** | Active prayer needs from the community |
| **Answered Prayers** | Testimonies of prayers that have been answered |

### Feed Features

- **🔍 Search** - Find prayers by keyword or person's name
- **📂 Category Filter** - Filter by category (Health, Family, Work, etc.)
- **🚨 Urgent Filter** - Show only urgent prayer requests
- **↻ Pull to Refresh** - Swipe down to refresh the feed

### Understanding Prayer Cards

Each prayer card shows:
- **Author's name** and profile picture
- **Prayer content** - The request or testimony
- **Category badge** - Color-coded category
- **🙏 Prayer count** - How many people have prayed
- **💬 Comment count** - Community encouragement
- **Time posted** - When it was shared

## Creating Prayer Requests

### How to Create a Prayer Request

1. Tap the **+ button** (orange floating button) on the home feed
2. **Write your prayer request** - Be as detailed as you're comfortable with
3. **Select a category**:
   - 🩺 Health
   - 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family
   - 💼 Work
   - 💰 Finances
   - 🤝 Relationships
   - 🙏 Spiritual
   - 🧭 Guidance
   - 🛡️ Protection
   - 🙌 Gratitude
   - 📝 Other

4. **Set options** (optional):
   - 🚨 **Mark as Urgent** - For critical needs
   - 🔒 **Make Private** - Only visible to your groups
   - 👤 **Post Anonymously** - Hide your name

5. Tap **Submit Prayer Request**

### Editing Your Prayer Requests

1. Open your prayer request by tapping on it
2. Tap the **Edit** button (pencil icon)
3. Make your changes
4. Tap **Save Changes**

### Deleting Your Prayer Requests

1. Open your prayer request
2. Tap **Edit**
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete**
4. Confirm deletion

> **Note:** Only you (and admins) can edit or delete your own content.

## Praying for Others

### How to Pray for Someone

1. Find a prayer request in the feed
2. Tap the **🙏 Pray** button
3. You'll feel a haptic confirmation
4. The prayer count increases

### What Happens When You Pray

- ✅ Your prayer is recorded
- 📊 Your stats update (prayer count, streak)
- 🏆 You may unlock achievements
- 🔔 The author may receive a notification

### Leaving Encouraging Comments

1. Tap on a prayer request to open it
2. Scroll to the comments section
3. Type your encouraging message
4. Tap **Send**

## Sharing Answered Prayers

When your prayer is answered, share it as a testimony!

### How to Create a Testimony

1. Tap the **+ button** while on the "Answered Prayers" tab
2. **Write your testimony** - Share what happened
3. Optionally **link to original prayer request**
4. Tap **Submit Testimony**

### Why Share Testimonies?

- 🌟 Encourages others going through similar situations
- 📣 Celebrates God's faithfulness
- 🏆 Unlocks testimony achievements
- 🙏 Inspires the community

## Prayer Groups

Groups let you create private prayer circles with family, friends, or your church.

### Creating a Group

1. Go to the **Groups** tab
2. Tap the **+ button**
3. Enter:
   - Group name
   - Description (optional)
   - Choose an emoji
4. Tap **Create Group**

### Managing Your Group (Owners Only)

As a group owner, you can:
- **Edit group details** - Name, description, photo
- **Add a group photo** - Personalize your group with an image
- **Invite members** - Share the invite code
- **Delete the group** - Removes it for everyone

### Changing Group Photo

1. Open your group
2. Tap the **pencil icon** to edit
3. Under "Group Photo", tap **Add Photo** or **Change Photo**
4. Select an image from your library
5. Tap **Save Changes**

### Joining a Group

1. Get an invite code from a group member
2. Go to **Groups** tab
3. Tap the **Enter** icon (join button)
4. Enter the 8-character invite code
5. Tap **Join Group**

### Sharing Group Invite

1. Open the group
2. Tap **Invite**
3. Share the code via text, email, or social media

### Posting in a Group

1. Open the group
2. Tap **Share Prayer Request**
3. Write your prayer and submit
4. Only group members can see it

## Settings & Reminders

### Notification Settings

Control what notifications you receive:
- **Prayer updates** - When someone prays for your request
- **Comments** - New comments on your content
- **Group activity** - Updates from your groups
- **Critical prayers** - Urgent requests from the community

### Prayer Reminders

Set daily reminders to pray:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Reminders**
2. **Enable reminders**
3. **Set time** - When you want to be reminded
4. **Choose days** - Which days of the week
5. **Custom message** (optional)

### Privacy Settings

- **Share Profile** - Allow others to see your profile
- **Anonymous Posting** - Default to anonymous
- **Location Sharing** - Show your location on posts

## Tips for Using Lift

### Best Practices

1. **Be Specific** - Detailed requests get more meaningful prayers
2. **Update Your Requests** - Let people know of progress
3. **Share Testimonies** - Encourage others with answered prayers
4. **Pray Daily** - Build your streak and habits
5. **Join Groups** - Connect with people you know
6. **Leave Comments** - Encourage those who are struggling

### Etiquette Guidelines

- ✅ Be respectful and encouraging
- ✅ Keep content appropriate for all ages
- ✅ Report inappropriate content
- ✅ Respect others' privacy
- ❌ Don't share others' personal information
- ❌ Avoid spam or promotional content

### Troubleshooting

**App not loading?**
- Check your internet connection
- Pull down to refresh
- Close and reopen the app

**Can't see prayer requests?**
- Make sure you're on the "Prayer Requests" tab
- Check if filters are active (clear them)
- Ensure you're signed in

**Notifications not working?**
- Check device notification settings
- Enable notifications in app settings
- Restart the app
